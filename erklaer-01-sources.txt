THE BURIED WORLD — EXPLAINER 01
Roman Concrete Heals Itself. Nobody Meant It To.

WHAT THIS FILM IS

A shorter, simpler retelling of the flagship film
"Rome's Concrete Is Still Healing Itself" (15:21, published 18 Aug 2026).
Two drawn figures work through the same material in dialogue.

It introduces NO new claim, NO new source and NO new research.
Every sentence is tied below to a numbered block of the long film, and the
literature for all of them is in one place:

  https://github.com/clcodemail1-lab/the-buried-world-sources/blob/main/episode-04-sources.txt
  https://github.com/clcodemail1-lab/the-buried-world-sources/blob/main/episode-04-method.txt

If a sentence here says more than the block it cites, that is an error in
this film, and we want to hear about it.


WHAT IS A PHOTOGRAPH AND WHAT IS A DRAWING

Backgrounds   generated images, carried over unchanged from the long film,
              labelled on screen throughout (EU AI Act, Article 50)
Figures       drawn in code (cut-paper style). Not generated, not people.
Schematics    drawn in code, labelled "schematic" in the frame. Two of them:
              the repair cycle, and tension/compression in a loaded beam.

No photograph is altered. Where the film shows a process, the process is
drawn as a drawing and is recognisable as one — a white line closing a crack
ON a photograph would be a claim about that photograph.
