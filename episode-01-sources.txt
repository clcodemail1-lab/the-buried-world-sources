WHY THIS LIST EXISTS
====================

Every image in the film carries a number in the bottom right corner. This list
resolves each one. Every claim that could be checked was checked before the film
was recorded, and the works those checks rest on are named at the end.

If something here is wrong, tell me and I will correct it and say so.


THE IMAGES
==========

All 48 shots were made for this film. No photographs of Goebekli Tepe, of finds
from the site, or of excavation work appear anywhere in it, and no third-party
images are used.

That is a deliberate choice, not a shortcut. Photographs of the site and its finds
are under copyright, and German law protects photographs of public-domain objects
in their own right. Rather than borrow what cannot be licensed, this channel draws
what the published record describes.

Two kinds of images appear, and the film keeps them apart on purpose:

  RECONSTRUCTION - AI-generated, marked "AI RECONSTRUCTION" on screen for as long
                   as it is visible, alongside the EU marking for AI-generated
                   content. Photographic-looking shots show place and find;
                   painted shots show people, actions and rituals, which are
                   always interpretation.

  DIAGRAM        - maps, the timeline and the section drawing. These were drawn
                   for this film, not generated. Image models place lettering
                   unreliably, and in a film whose central point is that these
                   people had no writing, invented script would be a factual
                   error.

MAPS AND DIAGRAMS - WHAT THEY REST ON
=====================================

Coordinates are real and linearly projected; the vertical is stretched by
1/cos(latitude) so distances hold in both directions. No coastlines or borders are
drawn - the frame shows rivers and sites, and so claims only what is established.

The map at 7:50 deliberately carries NO distance figure between Goebekli Tepe
and Karacadag. Published sources scatter between roughly 30 and 96 km, and a channel
that argues for precision should not state a number it cannot defend - not in
speech, and not through the back door of a map.

Dates on the timeline: Goebekli Tepe c. 9500 BCE (oldest layers, PPNA), first
writing c. 3200 BCE, Stonehenge c. 3000 BCE (first earthwork phase), Great Pyramid
c. 2560 BCE.

SOURCES AND FURTHER READING
===========================

Gresky, J., Haelm, J. & Clare, L. (2017): "Modified human crania from Goebekli
  Tepe provide evidence for a new form of Neolithic skull cult", Science Advances
  3(6), 28 June 2017. - The three carved skull fragments, one of them drilled.

Clare, L. and colleagues, on the formation of the mound deposits - German
  Archaeological Institute (DAI), Goebekli Tepe research project and its
  excavation blog "Tepe Telegrams". - The case against deliberate backfilling,
  made by the excavation team itself.

Heun, M. et al. (1997): "Site of Einkorn Wheat Domestication Identified by DNA
  Fingerprinting", Science 278, 1312-1314. - Cultivated einkorn traced to one wild
  population on Karacadag.

Benedict, P. (1980): survey report of the joint Istanbul-Chicago prehistoric
  survey of 1963, in which the pillar tops are recorded as two small Islamic
  cemeteries.

Hauptmann, H.: excavations at Nevali Cori, 1983-1991, Heidelberg. The site was
  flooded by the Ataturk Dam reservoir in 1992; the portable finds were rescued,
  the architecture was not.

Schmidt, K.: publications on Goebekli Tepe from 1995 onward, including his
  inversion of the usual order - that the cult made farming necessary rather than
  the other way round.

Tas Tepeler programme and Karahan Tepe - Turkish Ministry of Culture and Tourism,
  from 2019.

UNESCO World Heritage List: Goebekli Tepe, inscribed 2018.


WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM
============================

The title says "temple". The film says why that word is disputed and answers the
question in the open, at 11:30. Whether the enclosures were deliberately buried is
unresolved, and the film says so instead of choosing the better story.

"We don't know" is a finding, not a failure.
