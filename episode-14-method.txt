THE BURIED WORLD
Episode 14 - Nobody Counted The Small Ones

How this film was made, and what was corrected before recording

Last updated 17 September 2026


WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Every episode of this channel is checked line by line against the publications
before it is recorded, and the corrections are published. They are the most
useful thing here: they show where the ordinary version of a story is wrong,
and they show what this film got wrong first.

Seven things changed between the first draft and the recorded script. Two of
them were versions of the story that circulate widely and do not hold.


1. THE NINE FINGERS BELONG TO THE MONKEY, NOT TO THE HANDS

The draft said the plain carries "a pair of hands with nine fingers". The
nine-fingered figure that is actually documented is the MONKEY - spiral tail,
nine fingers. The separate figure known as the Hands has five fingers on one
hand and four on the other, which also comes to nine, and the two facts have
been merged in popular retellings until they are hard to separate.

Corrected to the attribution that is actually documented. The film now says
"a spider, a monkey with a spiral tail and nine fingers".


2. THE DATED POST, AND A CIRCULAR ARGUMENT

The draft said that wooden stakes at the ends of the straight lines "have been
radiocarbon dated, and they are as old as the lines they stand at".

Two problems. The plural overstates the evidence: what is documented is a
small number of posts, one of which was dated to around 525 CE. And the second
half of the sentence is circular - the lines were dated partly THROUGH such
posts, so the posts cannot then confirm the age of the lines.

Corrected to the single documented date, with no claim attached to it.


3. KOSOK WATCHED THE SUNSET THE DAY AFTER THE SOLSTICE

The widely repeated version has Paul Kosok standing on the pampa at the winter
solstice of 1941. The date usually given in the literature is 22 June 1941 -
the day AFTER the southern winter solstice.

This matters more here than it would elsewhere, because the whole passage is
about alignment claims and how easily they are made. The film now gives the
date and the one-day offset.


4. MARIA REICHE'S FIFTY YEARS

The draft said she "came to Peru as a governess and stayed for fifty years".
She arrived in 1932 and died in Peru in 1998 - sixty-six years. The round
number was a guess that had not been checked against either date.

Corrected to "came to Peru in nineteen thirty-two as a private tutor and never
went home".


5. HAWKINS: WHICH YEAR TO NAME

The dates for Hawkins's test scatter across the sources - the computation is
placed in 1967, the fieldwork in 1968, and the final report is dated 1969.
Under this channel's rule for scattered dates, the number gets replaced by the
named work rather than picked. The film now says the result was reported in
1969, and the source list gives the report.


6. THE RESERVE IS NOT THE SIZE OF TRINIDAD

The draft compared the 5,600 square kilometres of the archaeological reserve to
the island of Trinidad, which is 4,768 square kilometres - about fifteen per
cent out. Replaced by the English county of Norfolk (5,371 square kilometres),
which is both closer and more familiar to most of this channel's audience.


7. THE VISIBILITY STUDY SAYS SOMETHING BETTER THAN THE POPULAR VERSION

The draft used the line that circulates in popular accounts: that a survey of
one neighbouring area found "every geoglyph in it could be seen from ground
level". That is not what the study found, and the real finding is stronger.

Lambers and Sauerbier compared how much of the surrounding terrain the geoglyph
sites in the Palpa valleys can be seen from against the same measure for random
points in the same terrain, using a two-tailed Kolmogorov-Smirnov test. The
geoglyph sites came out significantly better. Their conclusion is that good
visibility was a criterion when a place was chosen for a new geoglyph - that
is, being seen from the ground was part of the decision.

The film now states the comparison and the conclusion, not the slogan.


WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT

- The further geoglyphs reported by the same team in 2025. That report was
  presented at an exhibition, not published in a journal. The film names it in
  the spoken text as a caution and does not count the figures.

- Ancient astronauts, in either direction. The film neither entertains the idea
  nor argues against it. A film that sets out to refute a claim ends up in the
  same place as the claim, and this channel has already learned that the
  expensive way. What is in the film instead is a finding: the figures are not
  only visible from the air, and the small ones were made to be seen by someone
  arriving on foot.

- Any casualty, ritual or religious claim attached to the severed-head motif.
  It appears in this film as what it is in the paper: a share of a motif count,
  32.9 per cent, and a plain outline in the ground.


HOW THE PICTURES WERE MADE

Every photograph-like shot in this film is generated, and the film says so on
screen throughout. 34 of the 91 shots are drawings - maps, timelines, charts -
made in code from the published figures, because a generated image cannot be
trusted to place a number, a boundary or a proportion correctly.

The geoglyph outlines in those drawings are schematic. They are not tracings of
the real figures and are not to be read as survey drawings; every panel that
carries one says so in its footer.

The full shot list, with the source of every claim, is in
folge-14-sources.txt.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
