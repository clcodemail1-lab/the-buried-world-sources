WHY THIS LIST EXISTS

This channel makes claims about the past using images that were generated by a
machine. That only works if you can check the claims. Everything the film states
is listed below with its source, and everything it could not verify was cut -
the companion file dispatch-12-method.txt sets out what was dropped and why,
including one case that was in the plan for weeks and turned out not to exist.


THE FOUR PUBLICATIONS THIS EPISODE RESTS ON

Giaccari, C., Romano, G., Soncin, S., Panella, S., Alhaique, F., D'Agostino, A.,
  Jaouen, K. and Tafuri, M. A. (2026). When collagen fails: Zinc isotopes unlock
  Sumerian lifeways in southern Mesopotamia. Proceedings of the National Academy
  of Sciences 123 (11), e2526276123. Published online 9 March 2026.
  https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2526276123
  ABSTRACT ONLY - no paywalled full text was used for this episode.
  -- Source of the zinc isotope method on human and
  faunal dental enamel at Abu Tbeirah, of the integration with carbon, oxygen
  and the Ba/Ca and Sr/Ca ratios, of the omnivorous diet based on C3 cereals and
  terrestrial animal products (likely including pigs) with limited freshwater
  resources, of the finding of "no or little evidence of marine fish
  consumption, despite the site's proximity to the ancient shoreline", and of
  the absence of dietary variation by sex within this nonelite population.

Zhou, Y., Chen, X., Wang, Y., Li, X., Fu, Y., Feng, E., Wang, L. and Wu, X.
  (2026). Differentiated provisioning in a mobile royal camp: zooarchaeological
  and stable isotope evidence from Houmingzi, a locality within a Liao-Jin
  spring nabo site complex, Northeast China. Archaeological and Anthropological
  Sciences 18 (9). Published online 28 August 2026.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-026-02559-6
  ABSTRACT ONLY.
  -- Source of the assemblage dominated by cattle, sheep and horses, of the
  taxon-specific mortality profiles and carcass-processing traces, and of the
  characterisation of the camp as a place that concentrated consumption while
  remaining spatially separated from the production that sustained it.

Testas, J., Matterne, V., Monteix, N., Amoretti, V., Ater, M., Belaj, A.,
  Blanchet, C., Bouby, L., Bonhomme, V., Comegna, C., Ivorra, S., Girard, V.,
  Kaniewski, D. and Kassout, J. (2026). Olive diversity in Pompeii: morphometric
  analysis of fuel-derived charred stones (c. 50-79 CE) from two bakeries.
  Journal of Archaeological Science 193, 106648. September 2026 issue; DOI
  registered 18 July 2026.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jas.2026.106648
  ABSTRACT ONLY - ScienceDirect refuses automated access.
  -- Source of the charred olive stones from the combustion structures of two
  Pompeian bakeries, of the outline analysis against modern cultivars, of the
  two dominant morphotypes alongside high morphological diversity, and of the
  reading that olive pressing waste was recycled as fuel for urban workshops.

Eber, J., Gur-Arieh, S., Power, R. C., Rageot, M. and Stockhammer, P. W. (2026).
  Ashes from Pompeii: incense burners, residue analyses and domestic cult
  practices. Antiquity 100 (411), 694-713. Published online 30 March 2026.
  https://doi.org/10.15184/aqy.2026.10320
  ABSTRACT ONLY.
  -- Source of the two censers (one from the Officina di Sabbatino at Pompeii,
  excavated 1954; one found in situ in the domestic shrine of a villa rustica at
  Boscoreale, 1986), of the identification of oak, laurel and stone-fruit wood,
  of the Burseraceae resin most likely from Canarium rather than frankincense,
  of the grape-derived product identified from the malic-to-tartaric acid ratio,
  and of the authors' own caution that post-depositional contamination cannot be
  excluded because no sediment control samples were preserved.


WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- It does not claim that elites at Abu Tbeirah ate differently from anybody
  else. Press coverage of that paper says so; the paper studied a NONELITE
  population and reports that diet did not vary by sex within it. An elite
  comparison would be a claim about something the study does not measure.
- It does not give a count of the olive stones, of the bones, or of the
  individuals sampled. Those numbers were not available from the sources
  consulted, and a number that cannot be checked is worse than none.
- It does not say what the resin was used for beyond burning, and it does not
  call the Roman ritual by its Latin name.
- It does not claim the grape product is certain. The authors say it is not, and
  the film says so in their terms.

TWO CORRECTIONS TO OUR OWN NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Both were found while checking the DOIs against the Crossref record, not while
reading our own file - which is the point of checking.

1. The olive stone paper is article 106648, not 106659. The wrong article
   number had been sitting in our stock of candidate topics since Dispatch 09
   and had been carried forward through three of them. A source list built on
   it would have pointed at a different paper.

2. The Pompeii incense paper is in Antiquity, not in the Journal of
   Archaeological Science. That was wrong in the same file.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATES: WHAT WAS CHECKED, FIELD BY FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

A journal record carries several dates and they do not agree. The issue label
is not the day a paper appeared. Each DOI below was read at Crossref with the
fields kept apart:

  DOI                            online         print issue     record created
  10.1073/pnas.2526276123        2026-03-09     2026-03-17      2026-03-09
  10.1007/s12520-026-02559-6     2026-08-28     2026-09         2026-08-28
  10.1016/j.jas.2026.106648      none           2026-09         2026-07-18
  10.15184/aqy.2026.10320        2026-03-30     2026-06         2026-03-30

The film therefore says "in March" for the two with a March online date, "in
August" for Houmingzi, and "in the September issue" for the olive stones - that
paper has no online date, so no day is claimed for it.

WHAT IS DRAWN AND WHAT IS GENERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Thirteen of the forty-nine shots are diagrams drawn in code. They carry no AI
label because nothing about them is generated, and that is also why their
labelling can be trusted with a number - a picture model cannot.

The diagrams state their own limits on the picture. The two maps say the
positions are approximate and that the ancient shoreline is an approximate
zone, not a surveyed line. The bar charts say they show that the patterns
differ, not by how much. The outline-analysis diagram says it illustrates the
method rather than reproducing the published one, and the scatter beside it
says the spread is the finding while the positions are not measured values.
The trade-route map says it shows origin regions, not routes. The caution on
the residue diagram is quoted from the publication; it is not an objection
raised by this film.

Two diagrams show blank ruled lines where ancient text would be. No ancient
passage is reproduced anywhere in this film, in any language.

Every photographic-looking shot is generated and none depicts a real,
identifiable find, site or person. No third-party images or footage are used,
so no third-party rights apply.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
