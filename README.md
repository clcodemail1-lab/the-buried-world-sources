# The Buried World — sources

Source lists and method notes for the YouTube channel
**[The Buried World](https://www.youtube.com/@theburied_world)** —
archaeology and the ancient world.

Every episode links here from its description. Two files per episode:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `*-sources.txt` | Every image in the film resolved by its on-screen number, every diagram explained, and the literature the fact-check rests on |
| `*-method.txt` | How the film was made — including the claims that were corrected or cut before recording |

## Why the corrections are published

The channel's position is to explain rather than to assert. A film that asks
you to weigh competing explanations owes you its own workings, including the
places where the first draft was wrong. The method notes say what changed and
why, rather than quietly absorbing it.

## Marking

Every film on this channel is AI-generated imagery, marked as such on screen
throughout and declared in the upload form. Nothing reproduces a specific
artefact, monument or excavation. The diagrams are drawn rather than
generated, because image models place lettering unreliably and an invented
figure on a map is still an invented figure.
