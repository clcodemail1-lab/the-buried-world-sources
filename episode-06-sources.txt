WHY THIS LIST EXISTS
====================

Every image in the film carries a number in the bottom right corner. This list
resolves each one. Every figure, name and date was checked against specialist
sources before the film was recorded, and the works those checks rest on are
named at the end.

Undeciphered scripts attract claimed solutions the way few subjects do. That
obliges a film about one to be unusually exact about its own footing. Where a
figure is contested, this list says so; where a figure was cut from the script
because the sources would not carry it, it says that too.

If something here is wrong, tell me and I will correct it and say so.


ABOUT THE SIGNS IN THE IMAGES
=============================

This matters more in this film than in any other on the channel, so it is
stated plainly:

  THE SIGNS IN THE GENERATED IMAGES ARE NOT LINEAR A.

An image model cannot write Linear A. It can only produce marks that look like
it. Every tablet, fragment and libation table in this film is a reconstruction,
and the marks on them are plausible linear strokes - not readable text, not a
transcription of any real inscription, and not usable for study.

That is why no generated image in this film shows an inscription large enough
or sharp enough to be read as a facsimile, and why everything the film actually
needs you to read is DRAWN rather than generated: the number system, the
account with ku-ro, the libation formula, and Ventris's grid. Those are set in
the Latin transcription that the scholarly literature uses - which is, in the
end, exactly what the film is about. What we have of Linear A is the
transcription.

The numerals shown at 00:00 are the one exception among the drawn material that
reproduces the original signs directly: the Linear A numeral signs are simple
and securely documented. The seventeen fraction signs are given only as a
count, because their values are still argued over.


THE IMAGES
==========

All 62 shots were made for this film. No photograph of any real tablet, of any
excavation, of any museum object and of any person appears anywhere in it, and
no third-party images are used. Photographs of public-domain objects are
protected in their own right under German law (BGH, Reiss-Engelhorn), so the
channel makes its own images instead.

Two shots are deliberately composed so that they are NOT a substitute for a
photograph of the real object: the Rosetta Stone is seen from behind at an
angle where its face cannot be read, and the rock face at Behistun is seen from
far enough away that the relief is only texture. Both show the situation, not
the document.

THE DIAGRAMS
============

  2:26  Linear A (c. 1800-1450 BC) against Linear B (c. 1450-1200 BC) and
         the decipherment of 1952. The asymmetry is the point: the younger
         script is read, the older one is not.
  2:50  Real coordinates, no coastlines. The figures are TABLET counts,
         not counts of inscribed objects - the two are regularly mixed up in
         the literature. Hagia Triada 147, Chania 99, Zakros 31, Phaistos 26,
         Knossos 6.
  4:14  Find spots outside Crete. No trade routes are drawn, because the
         find spots are documented and the routes are not.
  4:56  Ventris's grid, correctly shown WITHOUT sound values: the axes are
         labelled "consonant 1..6" and "vowel 1..5", because which consonant
         and which vowel was exactly what the grid did not yet say.
  6:24  The numeral signs. Units, tens, hundreds, thousands - and the
         seventeen fraction signs given only as a count.
  6:53  An account, schematically: commodity signs, quantities, and the
         line where ku-ro stands. The quantities in the diagram really do add
         up (31 + 18 + 24 + 12 = 85); the script that draws it asserts the sum
         before it will render.
  9:45  Linear B against Linear A on four counts, then the result.
  11:13  The libation formula: what holds on every stone, and the one
         position that changes. The changing slot is drawn as a BLANK, not
         filled with an invented name.
  14:51  The three things that would solve it, each with what it needs.


WHAT WAS CUT OR QUALIFIED
=========================

  1. The number of signs in the corpus. Sources give 7,362, 7,396 and "about
     7,400", depending on how damaged signs are counted. The film says "a
     little over seven thousand" and the thumbnail says 7,000 - a figure that
     is true on every count.
  2. Object counts per site. Hagia Triada is often given as 1,269 objects and
     147 tablets in the same paragraph. The film uses tablet counts only, and
     says so.
  3. The values of the fraction signs. Documented as a set of seventeen;
     individual values are still disputed and are not given.
  4. The meaning of the libation formula. (J)A-SA-SA-RA-ME has been read as a
     divine name, as the vessel itself and as "this dedication". The film says
     only that the formula recurs.
  5. Named authors of rejected decipherments. Attempts in Indo-Iranian,
     Semitic, Anatolian, Etruscan and Kartvelian directions are all real and
     are listed below, but the narration names nobody: the film's argument is
     about a pattern, not about people.
  6. Claimed AI decipherments of 2026. None has appeared in a peer-reviewed
     journal. Rather than repeat an unverifiable claim, the film states the
     test any decipherment has to pass.
  7. "67% of words". The 2019 paper reports 67.3% of correctly translated
     COGNATES. The draft said "words"; that overstates the result and was
     corrected.
  8. Linear A at Drama, Bulgaria. Attribution not consistent across sources;
     cut from the list of find spots.
