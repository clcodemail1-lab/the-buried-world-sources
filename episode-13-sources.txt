WHY THIS LIST EXISTS

This channel makes claims about the past using images that were generated by
a machine. That only works if you can check the claims. Everything the film
states is listed below with its source, and everything the film could not
verify was cut - the companion file episode-13-method.txt sets out the
corrections made before recording, including one where I had it exactly
backwards and had to rewrite an entire act.

WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- It does not claim the transmission route is known. Droplet spread between
  people and spillover from animals are the authors' readings, built from
  what the bacterium could not do, what the graves show, and what plague does
  today. Nobody watched it happen.
- It does not claim the ypm locus killed those children. The authors state
  these features MIGHT partly help explain the age pattern and that
  establishing the real effect would require functional studies that have not
  been done. The film says so in those terms.
- It does not give a death toll. The paper reports detection rates, not
  casualties. Any number would have been invented.
- It does not name a date for the end of the second outbreak phase in the
  spoken text. The published range 5315-4235 cal BP has a long tail because
  it rests on only two dates; the most likely range is about 5050-4850 cal BP.
  That is a qualification the narration could not carry without costing more
  time than the figure is worth, so the figure stays here instead.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE NOT

None of these images is a photograph of a real find. Every one of them was
generated or drawn for this film, and they stand for the things the film
describes rather than depicting particular ones. Where a specific site is
named in the commentary - Bratskii Kamen, the Angara, Smithfield - the image
beside it is a stand-in, not that place.

THIRTY-EIGHT of the 86 shots are drawn rather than generated, and that is the
highest share this channel has ever run. The reason is the subject. Detection
rates set against each other, pedigrees with carriers marked, two age
distributions, a phylogeny with a polytomy, three maps and two timelines are
statements an image model cannot make without inventing them. They are drawn
in Python, and every one of them carries its source in its own footer.

The drawn distributions are SCHEMATIC and say so on the image itself. The
curves illustrate what the paper reports - a mortality peak at 7.5 to 11 years
and the scarcity of deaths between 20 and 35 - and they carry no counts,
because the paper publishes none in that form.

The maps show ENDPOINTS and distances, not routes. The film does not claim a
migration path: the authors argue explicitly for a continent-wide rodent
reservoir rather than a continuous chain of human contact, and drawing a line
of travel would have contradicted the very finding the act rests on.


A NOTE ON WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

The victims of these outbreaks were mostly children between seven and eleven
years old. There is no depiction of a sick, dying or dead person anywhere in
this film - not photographic, not painted, not implied. Where the commentary
speaks about the children, the picture is a grave plan or an age
distribution, because a drawn grave number is precisely what survives of
them, and a painted face would be an invention at the one place where the
film can least afford one.

The shot that accompanies the line "there is nothing to illustrate here" is an
empty gravel terrace above a grey lake, and it carries no sound bed either.
