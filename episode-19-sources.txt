SOURCES AND LIMITS

One publication carries this film, and its main text was read in full: the
toxicological study of eight Inca sacrificial children from five Andean
volcanoes, published open access in Antiquity in November 2026. Its online
supplementary material (detection limits, chromatograms) was not read; nothing
in the film depends on it. The two earlier studies of the Llullaillaco
children by Wilson and colleagues (2007, 2013) were also read in full. The
Spanish chronicles of the conquest era (Cobo, Ramos, Acosta, Holguin and
others) were not read in the original; they are quoted only as these papers
cite them, and the film marks them as chronicle, not as finding. The debate
about returning the children comes from a 2022 news report (El Heraldo /
Telam). Two news reports of 3 November 2026 were read for the way the result
travelled; where they go beyond the paper, the film follows the paper. The
companion file folge-19-method.txt (published as episode-19-method.txt)
holds the complete fact-check table.

WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- That the children were sedated, or that they were calm and well. Both are
  readings by research teams (2013 and 2026); neither is a measurement.
- Coca "in all eight". The paper found cocaine in all six hair samples; the
  two nail samples showed none.
- That ayahuasca was drunk. One trace of harmine (0.6 pg/mg) in one nail
  sample, which the authors call contaminated, is not a drink.
- That a substance that was not found was never taken. A single drinking event
  leaves no trace in hair.
- How the children died. The paper does not say; the film does not ask.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE

None of the 83 shots shows a mummy, a body or the face of a child. Four are
real photographs, credited above: the volcanoes seen from space, the summit
of Llullaillaco twice, and a flower. The 46 generated shots - summits,
shrines, objects, laboratories and painted scenes - show places and things of
the TYPE described, not a published image and not a particular find; they are
marked as such on screen with the EU marking in the first fifteen seconds and
on every such shot. Where children appear in a painted scene they are small,
wrapped in mantles and seen from behind.

The 33 diagrams are drawn in code from the paper's text and Tables 1 and 2.
Where they show a schematic, they say so in their own footers; the maps place
the mountains at true coordinates and draw no coastline.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
