WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS
====================

A channel that asks you to trust it should show its working. This page says how
the film was put together, what was checked, what had to be corrected or cut
before recording, and where it stops short of an answer.

None of it is required of me. I would rather you could check.


WHY THERE ARE NO PHOTOGRAPHS
============================

Not one photograph of the Antikythera mechanism appears in this film. No
photograph of the fragments, of the wreck, or of any museum object.

That is a legal position as much as an editorial one. Photographs of the
fragments are protected, and under German law a photograph of a public-domain
object is itself protected for decades - being ancient does not make an image
free. So the film draws what the published record describes, and says on screen
that it is drawing.

The look of an image tells you what kind of claim it makes: photographic-looking
shots show PLACE AND FIND, painted shots show PEOPLE AND ACTIONS - everything a
human being is doing in this film is interpretation. The map, the timeline and
the three panels are DRAWN, not generated. An early generated attempt put Roman
numerals on the dial of a Greek machine - exactly the kind of quiet factual
error this channel exists to avoid.

All reconstructions are AI-generated and marked twice: the EU marking for
AI-generated content together with the words AI RECONSTRUCTION sit in the lower
left corner for as long as the image is on screen, and the marking appears once
more, large, between 0:03 and 0:15. From the next film onward the wording drops
away and the EU marking stands alone - it already says AI GENERATED, and
everything else is on this page.

The narration is a synthetic voice. The score was composed for this film.


WHAT THE FACT-CHECK CHANGED
===========================

Every figure, name and reading was checked against specialist sources before
recording. These points did not survive that check unchanged. A correction you
never see is worth nothing, so here they are.

1. The depth of the wreck became "more than forty metres".
   Published figures scatter between roughly 42 and 64 metres. A channel that
   argues for precision does not pick one - it states the floor.

2. The divers' names were cut from the narration.
   Elias Stadiatis found the wreck, Dimitrios Kontos brought up the arm,
   Georgios Kritikos died in the 1901 salvage. The film says what happened
   without asking a synthetic voice to carry Greek names it would mangle;
   the names belong here instead.

3. A precision figure for the lunar mechanism was cut.
   Secondary sources quote "better than 1 part in 200" for the pin-and-slot
   model of the Moon's varying speed. I could not trace the figure cleanly to
   the primary literature, so the film describes the mechanism and claims no
   number.

4. "More than three thousand characters" is a floor, not an estimate.
   Reports of the deciphered text scatter between 3,400 and 3,500 characters,
   so the film claims the lower bound.

5. The total gear count of the complete machine is not spoken.
   Thirty gears survive; reconstructions of the full machine range far wider,
   and the film does not choose among them.

6. The month of the 1902 discovery was cut; the year stands.


WHERE THE RESEARCH DOES NOT AGREE
=================================

Two questions in this film are open, and the film says so rather than choosing
the version that tells better.

WHO BUILT IT, AND WHERE?
  The month names engraved on the calendar are Corinthian, which points west -
  to Corinth or its greatest colony, Syracuse, the city of Archimedes. The
  astronomy in the gearing is the lunar theory of Hipparchus, who worked on
  Rhodes, where Cicero later saw such a device. The inscriptions lean west; the
  astronomy leans east. The film gives both readings and does not decide.

WHEN WAS IT MADE?
  The letter forms say 150-100 BCE. In 2014, Carman and Evans showed the
  eclipse tables fit best if the Saros dial's start date is 205 BCE. A start
  date is not necessarily a construction date - a dial can be set to an epoch
  older than itself - and the film leaves the question exactly there.

One more limit stated plainly: the planetary display on the front is a
reconstruction published in 2021. It is rigorous, and it is a hypothesis - of
the planetary gearing itself, not one wheel has been found. The film says this
in so many words.
