THE BURIED WORLD
Episode 11 - The Four Under The Stairs

How this film was made
Last updated 19 August 2026


WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Every episode of this channel goes through a fact-check before a single word
is recorded. That check regularly changes the film. This file lists what it
changed here, because a channel that argues with other people's captions has
no business hiding its own corrections.

Six corrections were made to the script of this episode. Two of them changed
whole passages.


THE TWO THAT CHANGED PASSAGES

1. FIORELLI TOOK OVER IN 1860, NOT 1863.

The first draft had Giuseppe Fiorelli taking charge of the excavation in
1863, the year of the first casts, because that is the year everyone
remembers. He was appointed in 1860; 1863 is the year he became director of
the Naples museum as well, and the year the method was first used on a human
cavity. His proposal to divide the city into nine numbered regions dates from
1858, before either. The passage was rewritten and his age corrected from 39
to 37.

2. FIORELLI'S "TWO WOMEN" ARE NOT THE TWO MAIDENS.

This is the correction that mattered most, and it took reading the primary
literature to catch. The first draft quoted Fiorelli's 1877 description of
"two women, one beside the other" and then, a few minutes later, reported
that DNA had shown one of that pair to be male. Those are two different
objects.

  - Fiorelli describes casts 2 and 3, found on 5 or 6 February 1863 in the
    Vicolo degli Scheletri. Neither has been sequenced. The current reference
    catalogue gives the sex of both as not definable.
  - The pair popularly known as the Two Maidens are casts 21 and 22, made in
    1914 from bodies found in the garden of the House of the Cryptoporticus.
    Cast 22 is the one shown to be male.

Merging them would have produced a clean, satisfying and false story. The
finished film keeps them apart: the 1877 description shows the pattern, the
1914 pair shows the case.


THE OTHER FOUR

3. NOT EVERY INDIVIDUAL UNDER THE STAIRS HAS A NUCLEAR GENOME.

The draft said flatly that none of the four is related to any other. The paper
is more careful: for cast 50 only a mitochondrial genome could be
reconstructed, which excludes a shared maternal line but not a relation
through the father. The film now says this in the same breath as the finding
itself, and the graphic carries it in its footnote.

4. "MORE THAN ELEVEN HUNDRED" BECAME "MORE THAN A THOUSAND".

The number of victims recovered at Pompeii is given as "over 1,000" in the
2024 paper; secondary sources scatter between 1,000 and 1,150. Under this
channel's rule for scattering figures, the film uses the wording of the
primary source and the graphics label the grid accordingly.

5. THE DATE IN JUNE 1974 IS A RANGE, NOT A DAY.

The four cavities in the House of the Golden Bracelet are recorded as found
between 3 and 6 June 1974. The draft named the third. The film now says
"June, nineteen seventy-four".

6. "BOTH WERE MEN" BECAME "AT LEAST ONE".

Several press reports state that both of the Two Maidens were men, aged
about 18 and 20. The ages come from CT estimates; the sex of cast 21 is not
established by nuclear data. The paper's own summary says the pair "included
at least one genetic male". The film follows the paper.


WHAT WAS LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE

- The percentage figures from the ancestry models (48-75 per cent Anatolian
  and Levantine, 26-45 per cent from the direction of Iran). They sit in the
  supplementary data and vary by model and individual; the film uses the
  authors' own summary wording instead.

- The dog from the House of Orpheus. It is the most famous cast of all and it
  carries nothing this film needs, and a dying animal is exactly the register
  this channel avoids.

- Any image of a person dying. The subject of this episode is an object made
  in a workshop, and the film shows it as one.


A NOTE ON THE PICTURES

Eleven images were rejected during production and regenerated. Two of the
rejections were substantive rather than cosmetic:

  - A courtyard scene of a Roman household came back with tomatoes in the
    baskets. Tomatoes reached Europe after 1492.
  - Several first attempts showed the casts standing upright like statues on
    plinths. Pompeii's casts are of people who were where they fell; they
    lie. The instruction to the image model was rewritten to say so, and the
    change is visible in every later image.

The full record, including the reasons for the other nine, is in the
repository.
