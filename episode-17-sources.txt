HOW THIS EPISODE WAS CHECKED

Every figure spoken in this film was traced to a publication, and the date
fields of each DOI were read individually, because a journal's print date is
an issue label and not the day a paper appeared. The statements that were cut
or rewritten during that check are described, one by one, in the methods file
that accompanies this list (see the last line of the sources below).

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE

4 of the 59 shots are reproductions of public-domain works: the Anthony Roll
of about 1546 (Pepys Library 2991, Magdalene College, Cambridge) and Holbein's
portrait of Henry VIII of 1537. They are not generated. They come from
Wikimedia Commons and are shown whole or cropped and otherwise unaltered. The
legal basis is stated in the methods file: both works are in the public domain,
and section 68 of the German Copyright Act removes any related right in a
reproduction. No image of the Mary Rose Trust was used.

The 16 diagrams are drawn in code. Where they show measurements or positions
that were not made for this film - the Solent, the hull on the seabed, the
counts of finds - they say "schematic" or "approximate" in their own footers.

The other 39 shots are generated images and are marked as such on screen with
the EU marking in the first fifteen seconds and on every such shot.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
