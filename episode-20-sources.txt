SOURCES AND LIMITS

One publication carries this film, and it was read in full: a study of the
rock-cut staircase tunnel at Arsameia on the Nymphaios, published open access
in the Journal of Roman Archaeology in November 2026. It is an interpretation,
not an excavation report, and the film says so. The 1963 excavation report and
the other works the study cites were not read in the original; where the film
uses them, it uses them as the study reports them. Context outside the study
(the excavators, Nemrut Dag, the end of the kingdom, Lucian's birthplace) was
checked against the sources listed below. The companion file
folge-20-method.txt (published as episode-20-method.txt) holds the complete
fact-check table.

  rock-cut staircase tunnel at Arsameia on the Nymphaios. Journal of Roman
  Archaeology, pp. 1-20 (online 19 November 2026). Open access, CC BY 4.0.
  doi:10.1017/S1047759426101196

  stations, the Great Cult Inscription and the lines quoted from it, the
  excavators' unease, the 51 + 39 Anatolian tunnels and their dating, the
  water and cult readings (Strabo, Pompey, Kurul, Amaseia, Karalar), and the
  four-part reading of trap, darkness, body and water. The study's own
  sources (Doerner and Goell 1963; Von Gall 1967; Bulut 2017, 2020; Brijder

WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- That the tunnel was a cult place rather than a water tunnel, or the other
  way round. The study reads it as a water tunnel that could also act in
  the cult; that reading is an interpretation.
- That the two travellers from Doliche existed. They are the study's thought
  experiment.
- That any ancient text mentions the tunnel. None does explicitly.
- That the king on the handshake relief above the tunnel is certainly
  Antiochos. The study names him; other descriptions name his father.
- That Antiochos is buried on Nemrut Dag. His burial chamber has not been
  found.
- That the tunnels were shrines of Mithras. The study rejects that reading.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE

10 of the 91 shots are real photographs from the study, credited above.
The 49 generated shots - landscapes, rock, tunnels, reliefs and painted
scenes - show places and things of the TYPE described, not a published image
and not a particular find; the generated reliefs are not the reliefs of
Arsameia. They are marked as such on screen with the EU marking in the first
fifteen seconds and on every such shot.

The 32 diagrams are drawn in code from the study's text. The plan of the
sacred road is our own simplified drawing, not a copy of a published plan;
the maps and the tunnel section are schematic and say so.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
