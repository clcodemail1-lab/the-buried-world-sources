WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS
====================

A channel that asks you to trust it should show its working. This page says how
the film was put together, what was checked, what had to be corrected before
recording, and where it stops short of an answer.

None of it is required of me. I would rather you could check.


WHY THERE ARE NO PHOTOGRAPHS
============================

Not one photograph of Goebekli Tepe appears in this film. No photograph of a find
from the site, and none of excavation work.

That is a legal position as much as an editorial one. Photographs of the site and
its objects are protected, and under German law a photograph of a public-domain
object is itself protected for decades - being old does not make an image free.
Borrowing them would mean either paying for licences this channel cannot yet
afford, or using them and hoping. Neither is a way to build something.

So the film draws what the published record describes, and says on screen that it
is drawing.


THE TWO KINDS OF IMAGE, AND WHY THEY LOOK DIFFERENT
===================================================

The look of an image tells you what kind of claim it makes. This is deliberate and
holds through the whole film:

  Photographic-looking shots show PLACE AND FIND - the mound, the pillars, the
  quarry, the skull fragments. These follow published descriptions and
  measurements.

  Painted shots show PEOPLE AND ACTIONS - carrying, building, gathering, burying.
  Everything a human being is doing in this film is interpretation, and it is
  painted so you can see that at a glance.

  Maps, the timeline and the section drawing are DRAWN, not generated. Image
  models place lettering unreliably. In a film whose central point is that these
  people had no writing, invented script on a stone would be a factual error, not
  a blemish.

All reconstructions are AI-generated and carry the EU marking for AI-generated
content plus the words AI RECONSTRUCTION, for as long as they are on screen.

The narration is a synthetic voice. The score was composed for this film.


WHAT THE FACT-CHECK CHANGED
===========================

Every figure, name and reading was checked against specialist sources before
recording. Six things did not survive that check. They are listed here because a
correction you never see is worth nothing.

1. "A medieval cemetery" became "two small Islamic cemeteries".
   That is how Peter Benedict described it in 1963. The looser word was mine.

2. A distance for Nevali Cori was removed entirely.
   The draft said "twenty kilometres away". I could not defend the figure, so it
   is gone; the film gives the excavation dates, the director and the flooding
   instead.

3. The unfinished pillar gained a weight: seven metres long, roughly fifty tonnes.
   The length alone does not convey the scale. The tonnage does.

4. "Around twenty further enclosures" became "at least twenty enclosures across
   the mound". The twenty are the total, not additional ones. The draft would have
   nearly doubled the site.

5. The distance to Karacadag was cut, and the 1997 study named instead.
   Published figures scatter between roughly 30 and 96 kilometres. A channel that
   argues for precision should not state a number it cannot defend - so it states
   none, in speech and on the map alike.

6. Karahan Tepe's distance was softened to "about forty kilometres".
   Sources give between 35 and 46.

One more change was made for caution rather than error: the film says there was
"not one domesticated field of grain anywhere we know of" rather than "anywhere on
earth". The evidence supports the first sentence. The second claims more than
anyone can know.


WHERE THE RESEARCH DOES NOT AGREE
=================================

Two questions in this film are open, and the film says so rather than choosing the
version that tells better.

WAS IT A TEMPLE?
  The title uses the word. The film answers the question in the open at 11:30: if a
  temple means a building used only for cult, where nobody lives, then probably
  not - cisterns, hearths and domestic debris have been found. If it means a place
  where people spent effort that no amount of food, warmth or shelter can explain,
  then without question. The word is doing two jobs and the film separates them.

WHY IS IT BURIED?
  Klaus Schmidt read the fill as deliberate: each enclosure closed by hand, basket
  by basket, a new one raised beside it. Lee Clare and colleagues - from the same
  excavation team - argue that much of the fill is displaced material that ran in
  from higher ground over a long time. The film gives both, in that order, and ends
  where the evidence ends.

There is also a limit worth stating plainly. Schmidt's famous inversion - that the
cult forced farming rather than the other way round - is an argument, not a
finding. Two things happening side by side in the same landscape does not tell you
which one caused the other, and wild grain was being processed at the site on a
large scale, which complicates the clean division the argument depends on. The film
says this too.


IF SOMETHING HERE IS WRONG
==========================

Tell me. Corrections get pinned under the video, and this page gets updated with a
note saying what changed and when. That costs nothing and is the only thing that
makes the rest of it worth anything.

"We don't know" is a finding, not a failure.
