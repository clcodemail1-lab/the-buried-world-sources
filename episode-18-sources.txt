HOW THIS EPISODE WAS CHECKED

Every figure spoken in this film was traced to the paper it comes from, and
the date fields of its DOI were read individually. The statements that were
cut or rewritten during that check are described, one by one, in the methods
file that accompanies this list (see the last line of the sources below).

WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- A route, a method or a crew for moving the stones. The paper gives none.
- A date for the erection. The paper: "their precise date remains unknown".
- That Brimham is proved beyond doubt. The paper: "most likely", "closest
  correspondence"; "proved" is the press release.
- That the marks at the foot of the northern stone are cup marks. The paper:
  "might conceivably".
- Any real photograph. Every photograph-like shot is generated and labelled.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE

None of the 57 shots is a photograph of the Devil's Arrows, of Brimham Rocks,
of Plumpton Rocks, of the Battle Cross or of the laboratory. The 38
photograph-like shots are generated images of the TYPE described - a grooved
gritstone pillar in a field, a moorland tor, a tape on a stone - and are marked
as such on screen with the EU marking in the first fifteen seconds and on every
such shot. The scenes of antiquaries and of prehistoric people are painted
and marked the same way.

The 19 diagrams are drawn in code. Where they show an age distribution, a
map or a spacing, they say "schematic" or "positions approximate" in their own
footers; the maps place towns and outcrops at true coordinates and draw no
coastline and no river course.

Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
