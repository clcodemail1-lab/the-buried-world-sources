WHY THIS LIST EXISTS

This channel makes claims about the past using images that were generated by
a machine. That only works if you can check the claims. Everything the film
states is listed below with its source, and everything the film could not
verify was cut - the companion file episode-14-method.txt sets out the seven
corrections made before recording, including two where the common version of
a story turned out to be wrong.


THE PUBLICATIONS THIS EPISODE RESTS ON

Sakai, M., Sakurai, A., Lu, S., Olano, J., Albrecht, C. M., Hamann, H. F. and
  Freitag, M. (2024). AI-accelerated Nazca survey nearly doubles the number of
  known figurative geoglyphs and sheds light on their purpose.
  Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 121 (40), e2407652121.
  Published online 23 September 2024; October 2024 issue.
  https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2407652121
  -- Read in full. Source of the 303 newly identified figurative geoglyphs
  found in a six-month field season; of the roughly 430 figurative geoglyphs
  known from the preceding century of survey; of the rise in relief-type
  geoglyphs from 380 to 683; of the mean sizes (relief type about 9 m, line
  type about 90 m); of the motif proportions (relief type 33.8 per cent
  humanoids, 32.9 per cent severed heads, 14.9 per cent domesticated
  camelids, 81.6 per cent human-related in total; line type 64 per cent wild
  animals); of the mean distances (relief type 43 m from ancient trails, line
  type 34 m from the linear and trapezoidal network); of the dating (line
  type within the Nasca period, 100 BCE to 650 CE, relief type associated
  with the Initial Nasca period or earlier); of the model figures (47,410
  candidate locations, 1,309 ranked as high potential, about 1,200 labour
  hours of evaluation, 36 model suggestions per confirmed geoglyph); and of
  the authors' reading that relief-type figures shared information with
  individuals or small groups while line-type figures were built and used by
  the community for ceremonial purposes.

Sakai, M., Lai, Y., Olano Canales, J., Hayashi, M. and Nomura, K. (2023).
  Accelerating the discovery of new Nasca geoglyphs using deep learning.
  Journal of Archaeological Science 155, 105777. July 2023 issue; DOI
  registered 31 May 2023.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jas.2023.105777
  -- Source of the method: a detection model trained on known geoglyphs and
  applied to high-resolution aerial imagery of ground not previously examined
  in detail.

Lambers, K. and Sauerbier, M. (2006). GIS-based visibility studies of the
  Nasca geoglyphs at Palpa, Peru. In: Proceedings of the CAA conference.
  -- Source of the visibility finding: geoglyph sites in the Palpa valleys are
  visible from significantly more of the surrounding terrain than random
  points in the same terrain (two-tailed Kolmogorov-Smirnov test), from which
  the authors conclude that good visibility was a criterion in choosing where
  to place a new geoglyph. Also the source of the statement that the figures
  are NOT only visible from the air, which the authors call a false
  impression created by the way the site is visited today.

Hawkins, G. S. (1969). Ancient Lines in the Peruvian Desert. Final Scientific
  Report for the National Geographic Society expedition. Cambridge, Mass.:
  Smithsonian Institution, Astrophysical Observatory.
  -- Source of the computer test of 186 Nazca lines against the positions of
  sun, moon, planets and stars, and of the result that roughly a fifth showed
  an astronomical orientation - the proportion chance alone would produce.


THE EVENTS OF 2025

The reduction of the Nazca archaeological reserve from 5,600 to 3,200 square
kilometres was announced at the end of May 2025 and repealed by Peru's
Ministry of Culture on 8 June 2025, restoring the full area. Reported at the
time by, among others, the Washington Post, the Art Newspaper and
Mining Technology, and confirmed by the ministry's own announcement.


WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT CLAIM

- It does not claim the meaning of the figures is known. It does not claim
  the purpose question has been answered. The film's argument is that the
  question assumes a single answer for what turns out to be at least two
  different practices.
- It does not treat the identification of motifs as settled. The authors say
  so themselves: a worn nine-metre outline on a slope is an interpretation,
  and the percentages depend on those calls.
- It does not use the further geoglyphs reported by the same team in 2025.
  That report was made at an exhibition rather than in a journal. The film
  says this in the spoken text and does not count the figures.
- It does not say who made the relief-type figures or why. The authors offer
  a reading that follows from where the figures sit; the film gives it as
  their reading.
- It does not discuss ancient astronauts, and it does not argue against them.
  The film is about what the survey found and what the sample was missing.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE NOT

None of these images is a photograph of a real find. Every one of them was
generated or drawn for this film, and they stand for the things the film
describes rather than depicting particular ones. Where a specific site is
named in the commentary - Bratskii Kamen, the Angara, Smithfield - the image
beside it is a stand-in, not that place.

THIRTY-EIGHT of the 86 shots are drawn rather than generated, and that is the
highest share this channel has ever run. The reason is the subject. Detection
rates set against each other, pedigrees with carriers marked, two age
distributions, a phylogeny with a polytomy, three maps and two timelines are
statements an image model cannot make without inventing them. They are drawn
in Python, and every one of them carries its source in its own footer.

The drawn distributions are SCHEMATIC and say so on the image itself. The
curves illustrate what the paper reports - a mortality peak at 7.5 to 11 years
and the scarcity of deaths between 20 and 35 - and they carry no counts,
because the paper publishes none in that form.

The maps show ENDPOINTS and distances, not routes. The film does not claim a
migration path: the authors argue explicitly for a continent-wide rodent
reservoir rather than a continuous chain of human contact, and drawing a line
of travel would have contradicted the very finding the act rests on.


A NOTE ON WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

The victims of these outbreaks were mostly children between seven and eleven
years old. There is no depiction of a sick, dying or dead person anywhere in
this film - not photographic, not painted, not implied. Where the commentary
speaks about the children, the picture is a grave plan or an age
distribution, because a drawn grave number is precisely what survives of
them, and a painted face would be an invention at the one place where the
film can least afford one.

The shot that accompanies the line "there is nothing to illustrate here" is an
empty gravel terrace above a grey lake, and it carries no sound bed either.
