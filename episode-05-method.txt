THE BURIED WORLD
Episode 5 - They Smashed These Faces Before Burying Them

How this film was made


THE RULE
========

The title is allowed to sharpen. The film is not allowed to leave a claim
standing that it cannot support. Anything the sources will not carry gets cut,
and this page says what was cut and why. That is the whole method.


WHAT THE FACT-CHECK CHANGED
===========================

Four corrections and six cuts, made before a word was recorded.

Corrections:

  The pupils on the great mask are 16 cm long, not 17. A first draft had the
  wrong figure from a secondary source.

  Pit Two was found on 14 August 1986, four weeks after Pit One and twenty
  metres away. The draft said "six weeks later, a few metres away" - wrong on
  both counts.

  "No graves." That is false: a cemetery is under excavation at Renshengcun.
  What is true is narrower - no royal tombs have been identified. This one
  matters, because "no writing, no graves" had been drafted as the thumbnail
  line. A hook that the film has to qualify twice is not a hook, so it was
  replaced.

  "No writing." Also too broad. There are incised signs on a gold staff and on
  pottery. What is missing is a readable system, and that is what the film
  says.

Cuts:

  The number of elephant tusks. Sources say "over 100", "over 700" and "more
  than 1,000". A figure the sources cannot agree on is not a figure. The film
  says "hundreds".

  All dating figures except one. Earlier ranges circulate (1100-1050 BC among
  others). The film uses only the September 2025 result, with its interval and
  its stated probability, because that is the study designed to settle it.

  "The bronze tree is over four metres tall." It is 396 cm as restored, and the
  top is still missing - a fact worth having, since the height is a restoration
  and not a measurement of the original.

  The percentage split between the two groups of bronze figures. It circulates
  as 25/75; the underlying reading is an interpretation, not a count.

  "The civilisation vanished in a flood." Not supported. Act five argues the
  opposite, with Jinsha as the evidence.

  A "war and exodus" collapse thesis from 2025 press coverage. One source, no
  study named, conjunctive throughout. It is covered anyway by the political
  reading the film does give.


THE ONE THAT GOT THROUGH THE GATE
=================================

The script said Anyang lies "two thousand kilometres" north-east. It does not.
Sanxingdui to Anyang is about 1,100 km in a straight line - I know that because
I drew the map afterwards and the two points would not sit where the sentence
implied.

The figure had passed the fact-check because it was checked as a claim about
lead isotopes, which is sound, and the distance rode along with it unexamined.
The lesson is not "check harder". It is that a drawn map is itself a check: it
forces a number to be true in two dimensions at once, and a wrong one stops
fitting. The line now says "more than a thousand kilometres", and the map says
the same.


WHAT IS RECONSTRUCTED AND WHAT IS DRAWN
=======================================

Every image was made for this film. None of them is a photograph of the site or
of a museum object, and none is borrowed - photographs of public-domain objects
are still protected in Germany, so this channel draws what the record describes
rather than using what it cannot licence.

The film keeps two kinds of image apart on purpose. Photographic-looking shots
show place, find and laboratory. Painted shots show people and actions, because
those are always interpretation - the night the pits were filled, the breaking
of a mask, the emptied sanctuary. Every AI image carries the EU marking and the
words AI RECONSTRUCTION for as long as it is on screen. The eleven diagrams are
drawn in code from published coordinates and figures; they carry no AI marking
because nothing about them is generated.

No bronze in this film copies a specific museum object. The shapes follow the
published descriptions, because that is what the record supports.


WHERE THE FILM STOPS
====================

Act four gives four explanations and picks none. That is not fence-sitting. The
process is in the ground in great detail - the breaking, the burning, the
sorting, the layering, the single act proved by fragments that fit across three
pits. The intention is not in the ground at all, and no amount of confidence
puts it there.

If you want to know what a lack of evidence looks like when someone is being
straight with you: it looks like act two.
