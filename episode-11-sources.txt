WHY THIS LIST EXISTS

This channel makes claims about the past using images that were generated by
a machine. That only works if you can check the claims. Everything the film
states is listed below with its source, and everything the film could not
verify was cut - the companion file episode-11-method.txt sets out the
corrections made before recording, including two that changed whole passages.


THE PUBLICATIONS THIS EPISODE RESTS ON

Pilli, E., Vai, S., Moses, V. C., Morelli, S., Lari, M., Modi, A., Diroma,
  M. A., Amoretti, V., Zuchtriegel, G., Osanna, M., Kennett, D. J., George,
  R. J., Krigbaum, J., Rohland, N., Mallick, S., Caramelli, D., Reich, D. and
  Mittnik, A. (2024). Ancient DNA challenges prevailing interpretations of the
  Pompeii plaster casts. Current Biology 34, 5307-5318, 18 November 2024.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2024.10.007
  -- Source of everything the film says about the genomes: that bone was
     sampled from 14 casts and 5 yielded genome-wide data (casts 22, 25, 51,
     52 and 53); that all four individuals from the House of the Golden
     Bracelet are genetically male, including the adult wearing the bracelet;
     that no relatedness to the third degree was found between any of them;
     that for cast 50 only a mitochondrial genome could be reconstructed, so
     a relation through the paternal line cannot be excluded; that cast 22 of
     the pair known as the Two Maidens is male and carries a different
     mitochondrial haplogroup from cast 21; that cast 25 from the Villa of
     the Mysteries is male, with strontium and oxygen values consistent with
     the southern Campanian plain; that all individuals derive their ancestry
     largely from recent immigrants of the eastern Mediterranean; and of the
     coverage and contamination figures quoted in the film (median 0.054 on
     the 1240K set, contamination measurable for two individuals, both under
     four per cent). It is also the source for the counts of 104 casts and
     over 1,000 recovered victims.

Lazer, E. et al. Inside the casts of the Pompeian victims: results from the
  first season of the Pompeii Cast Project in 2015. Papers of the British
  School at Rome.
  -- Source of the conservation and imaging programme: 86 casts conserved, 26
     scanned or X-rayed, none containing a complete skeleton; of the metal
     rods found inside figures; of the frequent removal of bone before the
     plaster was poured; and of the finding that the casts had been
     considerably manipulated and likely creatively restored, with stylistic
     differences reflecting the period that made them. Summarised in the
     introduction of Pilli et al. 2024, from which the film quotes.

Osanna, M. et al. (2021), the current reference catalogue of the casts.
  -- Source for the statement that the sex of casts 2 and 3 - the pair
     Fiorelli described in 1877 - is today given as not definable.

Fiorelli, G. (1877). Guida di Pompei.
  -- Source of the quoted description of casts 2 and 3.

Settembrini, L. Letter to Giuseppe Fiorelli, 13 February 1863.
  -- Source of the phrase quoted in the film about the pain of death taking
     on body and form.

Parco Archeologico di Pompei (2026). Permanent display of the casts,
  Palestra Grande, opened 12 March 2026.
  -- Source for the number of casts on display (22), for the presence of CT
     images beside the figures, for the genetic result appearing in the wall
     text of the House of the Golden Bracelet group, and for the director's
     statement that the casts are not works of art but human testimony.


WHAT THE FILM DELIBERATELY DOES NOT CLAIM

The film does not say that the two figures in the House of the Cryptoporticus
were unrelated in life, only that they were not sisters and did not share a
maternal line - the third old proposal, that they were lovers, is untouched by
the data and the film says so.

The film does not give the number of victims as a precise figure. The
literature gives "over 1,000"; the graphics show a grid of 1,000 and label it
as such.

The dates of repair on the timeline in shot 42 stand for the practice, not for
one documented cast. The imaging shows plaster of different ages inside single
figures; the restoration work itself was not recorded.

WHAT THE IMAGES ARE NOT

None of these images is a photograph of a real cast in Pompeii. Every one of
them was generated for this film, and they stand for the objects the film
describes rather than depicting particular ones. Where a cast number is named
in the commentary, the image beside it is a stand-in, not that object.

No image in this film shows a person dying, a skeleton, blood, or ash falling
on anybody. The eruption appears once, in shot 10, and is shown through its
consequence - a street ending in a wall of hardened ash. The casts are shown
as what they physically are: poured gypsum plaster, in workshops, stores,
crates and cases. That is a deliberate limit, not an oversight.

Four shots are drawn rather than generated because their subject is a text:
[24] and [25] set the wording of the 1877 guidebook and of the 2021 catalogue,
[73] sets the sentence the geneticists wrote about restorers and poses, and
[80] sets the wall text of the display. Generated writing would have produced
plausible-looking nonsense in exactly the places where the wording matters.

One image was rejected during production for an anachronism - a courtyard
scene in which the baskets held New World produce - and regenerated. The full
record of rejected images is in the repository.

Every image carries a permanent AI label, and the official EU marking is
shown in full between 0:03 and 0:15.


Corrections are welcome and will be made out loud.
